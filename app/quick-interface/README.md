## Introduction

Quick Interface is a Blender addon that helps you manage your workspace areas with hotkeys. You can switch, split, duplicate, join, and swap areas using convenient pie menus. This addon is perfect for anyone who wants to improve their workflow and productivity in Blender.

## Features

- Switch Area
- Split Area
- Duplicate Area
- Join Area
- Swap Area

## Workflow

- `SHIFT`+`SPACE` — Switch Area
- `SHIFT`+`CTRL`+`SPACE` — Split Area
- `ALT`+`SPACE` — Duplicate Area
- `SHIFT`+`ALT`+`SPACE` — Join Area
- `SHIFT`+`CTRL`+`ALT`+`SPACE` — Swap Area

## Switch Area

Switch between workspace areas using a pie menu.

![Switching between workspace areas with pie menu](https://b3dhub.github.io/addons/images/quick-interface/area_switch.gif)

## Split Area

Split the current area vertically or horizontally.

![Splitting workspace area with pie menu](https://b3dhub.github.io/addons/images/quick-interface/area_split.gif)

Split Area hotkeys:

- `MOUSE WHEEL UP` — Split Vertically
- `MOUSE WHEEL DOWN` — Split Horizontally
- `CTRL` — Split Snap

## Duplicate Area

Duplicate the current area to a new window.

![Duplicating workspace area to new window](https://b3dhub.github.io/addons/images/quick-interface/area_duplicate.gif)

## Join Area

Join two areas into one.

![Joining two workspace areas into one](https://b3dhub.github.io/addons/images/quick-interface/area_join.gif)

## Swap Area

Swap the positions of two areas.

![Swapping positions of two workspace areas](https://b3dhub.github.io/addons/images/quick-interface/area_swap.gif)
