## Introduction

Quick Decal is a powerful Blender addon that allows you to effortlessly add decals to your models without the need for extra geometry. It uses a controller empty that blends with your existing material, ensuring a seamless integration with your model. This addon supports both image and PBR decals, as well as Quixel Megascans decals, and offers a range of features such as layers, multiple objects, and more. With Quick Decal, you can add decals to your models in EEVEE and Cycles with ease.

> Disclaimer: Users who have `claimed` or `purchased` Megascans assets through Quixel Bridge can use this add-on.

## Image Decal

Add custom image decals to your models. You can add a custom decal library path in the add-on preferences, then load decals from the `Decals` panel in the `Custom` section.

![Adding custom decal library path in preferences](https://b3dhub.github.io/addons/images/quick-decal/custom_decals.gif)

Load decals from the custom decal library.

![Loading custom image decals from library](https://b3dhub.github.io/addons/images/quick-decal/quick_decal_custom_load_add.gif)

## Quixel Megascans Decal

Add Quixel Megascans decals to your models. You can add the Megascans library path in the add-on preferences. The add-on reads the `assetsData.json` file to load the decals in the `Megascans` section of the `Decals` panel.

Download assets from [Quixel Bridge](https://d2shgxa8i058x8.cloudfront.net/bridge/win/Bridge.exe).

![Adding Quixel Megascans decals to models](https://b3dhub.github.io/addons/images/quick-decal/megascans_decals.gif)

![Browsing and loading Megascans decals from library](https://b3dhub.github.io/addons/images/quick-decal/megascans_decals_2.gif)

## Multiple Objects and Materials

Add decals to multiple objects and materials at once. Hold `Shift` to add the decal to all objects, hold `Ctrl` to add the decal to all materials of the active object.

![Adding decals to multiple objects and materials](https://b3dhub.github.io/addons/images/quick-decal/multiple_obj_mat_decal.gif)

Hotkeys: `Shift` and `Ctrl`

![Hotkeys for adding decals to multiple objects and materials](https://b3dhub.github.io/addons/images/quick-decal/hotkeys.png)

## Quick Baker Compatible

Quick Decal is compatible with [Quick Baker](https://superhivemarket.com/products/quick-baker). You can bake decal layers with Quick Baker.

![Baking decal layers with Quick Baker](https://b3dhub.github.io/addons/images/quick-decal/quick_decal_baker.gif)

## Caution

Do not edit or remove the `QDecal Master` node group. It is the core of the addon and editing it will break the addon.

![QDecal Master node group in shader editor](https://b3dhub.github.io/addons/images/quick-decal/master_node_group.png)
